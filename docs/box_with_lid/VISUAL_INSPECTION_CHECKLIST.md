# Visual Inspection Checklist: Box With Lid

Supplements the workspace-wide criteria in
[`docs/VISUAL_INSPECTION_CHECKLIST.md`](../VISUAL_INSPECTION_CHECKLIST.md). Items are qualitative
visual conditions; dimensions are validated by unit tests.

## 1. Lid Fit

- [ ] **Lid Seated Flush**: The lid sits flat on the box's wall tops all the way round, with its
  outside edges in line with the box's.
- [ ] **Lip in Groove**: In a hidden-line view, the box's lip sits inside the lid's groove with a
  visible gap on both sides and above it.

## 2. Screws and Nuts

- [ ] **Driven From the Top**: All four screw heads are on the lid's top face, sitting flush in
  their counterbores.
- [ ] **Aligned Corners**: Each screw passes straight down through the lid into the centre of its
  corner boss, through its nut.
- [ ] **Nut Slots Face Inward**: Each nut slot opens from the boss's inner face into the box's
  interior, never towards the lid or the outside.
- [ ] **Bridge Over Each Slot**: A thin disc spans the screw hole at the roof of each nut slot.

## 3. Printability

- [ ] **Box Builds Upward**: Printed floor-down, the cavity, bosses and lip all rise straight up
  from the floor with nothing overhanging.
- [ ] **Lid Groove on the Bed**: Printed underside-down, the lid's groove opens onto the bed and
  its counterbores open upward.
