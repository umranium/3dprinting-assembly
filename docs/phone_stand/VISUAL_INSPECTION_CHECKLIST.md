# Visual Inspection Checklist: Phone Stand

Supplements the workspace-wide criteria in
[`docs/VISUAL_INSPECTION_CHECKLIST.md`](../VISUAL_INSPECTION_CHECKLIST.md). Items are qualitative
visual conditions; dimensions are validated by unit tests.

## 1. Phone Fit

- [ ] **Phone Rests on the Backrest**: The phone's back face lies flat along the backrest's front
  face with no visible gap or tilt mismatch.
- [ ] **Phone Stands in the Groove**: The phone's bottom edge sits on the groove floor, behind the
  lip, with a visible gap between the phone and the lip.
- [ ] **Buttons Clear**: The phone overhangs the stand on both sides, so nothing covers its side
  edges.

## 2. Stand Structure

- [ ] **Closed Triangle**: Base, backrest and rear strut join into one closed triangle around an
  open window, with no gaps at the corners.
- [ ] **Stable Footprint**: The base runs well behind the phone's lean, giving a visibly wide stance.
- [ ] **Two-Colour Lip**: The lip shows in its own colour, flush with the base's front edge and
  sitting directly on the base with no step or gap.
- [ ] **Rounding Across the Colour Seam**: The rounded and chamfered edges on the front face and end
  faces run unbroken from the base into the lip, with no notch or step at the colour change.

## 3. Printability

- [ ] **Constant Profile**: Seen from the end, the side profile is the same along the stand's full
  width, so it can print lying on that end face with nothing overhanging.
